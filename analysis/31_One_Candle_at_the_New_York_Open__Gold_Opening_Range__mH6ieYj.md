# One Candle at the New York Open: Gold Opening Range

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Opening Range Breakout (ORB) / Multi-Timeframe Trend Alignment — Trading the initial 15-minute New York opening range on Gold (XAUUSD).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** At 9:30 AM New York time (US stock market cash open), volatility and trading volume in Gold surge dramatically. Instead of predicting the pre-market direction, this strategy establishes a clean, mechanical two-line boundary derived from the first 15-minute candle's extreme High and Low. By strictly aligning trades with the Higher Timeframe (4H) trend, false breakout traps are filtered out, capturing the primary session expansion leg.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Higher Timeframe Trend Filter (4H Chart):**
   - Assess the dominant trend on the 4-Hour chart.
   - If Gold is printing consistent Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL) beneath a descending trendline, adopt an exclusively **Bearish / Sell-Only** bias (and vice versa for an uptrend).
2. **Step 2: Define the Opening Range (15M Chart at 9:30 AM NY):**
   - At exactly 9:30 AM New York time, monitor the first 15-minute candle.
   - Once it closes (at 9:45 AM NY), mark two horizontal levels:
     - **Range High:** The exact high wick of that first 15M candle.
     - **Range Low:** The exact low wick of that first 15M candle.
3. **Step 3: Monitor the Breakout (5M Chart):**
   - Switch down to the 5-Minute timeframe to monitor execution price action.
   - **Filter Wicks:** A 5-minute wick that pokes below the Range Low and immediately snaps back is rejected as mere liquidity noise.
   - **Requirement:** Wait for a 5-minute candle to print a full **body close outside the range** (below the Range Low for sells).
4. **Step 4: Trade Entry:**
   - **Standard Entry:** Enter on the retest as price retraces upward to tap the broken 15M Range Low from below.
   - **Displacement Confirmation Entry:** If the breakdown candle closes decisively below both the Range Low and the most recent 5-minute swing low, enter immediately on the close/open of the following candle.
5. **Step 5: Structural Risk Management:**
   - Establish the stop-loss anchor and profit target according to the session plan.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Aligned strictly with the 4H trend (Bearish/Short when 4H is downtrending).
- **Entry Trigger:** 5M candle body close below the 15M Range Low (plus retest of broken level).
- **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed securely above the High of the 15M Opening Range (or above the local breakout pivot).
- **Trade Invalidation / Early Exit:**
  - If, after breaking out, a subsequent candle prints a **body close back inside the Opening Range**, the breakout idea is officially **cancelled**. Traders should exit or cut risk immediately, as failure to hold outside typically results in a sharp reversal running to the opposite extreme.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - Target a predefined lower-timeframe demand/support zone below, typically delivering a minimum of **2x Risk (2R)**.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Wick Trap:** Never execute during the active formation of a 5-minute candle that is piercing the range boundary. Wicks routinely probe beyond the opening range to sweep liquidity before violently reversing. The body close outside is mandatory.
- **Trend Alignment Rule:** Do not take breakouts against the 4H trend. If the 4H chart is down, ignore upside breakouts of the 15M range (which are often liquidity grabs) and wait exclusively for downside breaks.
- **"Close Back Inside" Failure Mode:** When price closes back inside the range after a breakout attempt, it indicates trapped breakout traders. Price will rapidly rotate to target the opposite boundary's stop losses.
