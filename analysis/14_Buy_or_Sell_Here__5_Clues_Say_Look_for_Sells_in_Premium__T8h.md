# Buy or Sell Here? 5 Clues Say Look for Sells in Premium

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (ICT / Premium vs. Discount Dealing Ranges, Liquidity Pools, Liquidity Sweeps, CHoCH, and Bearish Order Blocks).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  Following a prolonged parabolic rally, retail traders frequently get caught FOMO buying near the top or trying to guess reversals without structural backing. This strategy breaks down an institutional short framework that identifies when price is situated in Premium pricing and aligns five structural clues to target a return into Discount pricing.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

1. **Clue 1: Define the Range (Premium vs. Discount)**
   - Measure the complete dealing range by marking the major swing low and swing high of the expansion leg.
   - Divide the range at the 50% equilibrium level:
     - Above 50%: **Premium** (expensive; look for selling opportunities).
     - Below 50%: **Discount** (cheap; look for buying opportunities).
   - Because price sits high in the Premium zone, high-probability setups favor shorts.

2. **Clue 2: Change of Character (CHoCH)**
   - Price breaks down and prints a full candle close below the most recent swing low.
   - This structural break signals an early transition of power from buyers to sellers.

3. **Clue 3: Identification of Equal Highs (Liquidity Engineering)**
   - Price pulls back upward and forms relatively equal highs (EQH).
   - Retail traders view this as standard horizontal resistance and enter early shorts, placing their stop-loss buy orders just above these peaks. This engineers a dense pool of buy-side liquidity.

4. **Clue 4: The Liquidity Sweep**
   - Price surges briefly above the equal highs, triggers and absorbs the retail stop losses, and quickly closes back below the level.
   - The rejection wick confirms that this was not a breakout, but an institutional sweep of resting liquidity.

5. **Clue 5: The Bearish Order Block (OB)**
   - Locate the last bullish (up) candle before the original displacement drop that caused the CHoCH.
   - When price sweeps liquidity and taps into this Order Block, institutional sellers re-enter the market to defend their short positions.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bearish continuation / mean reversion from Premium into Discount.
- **Entry Trigger:** Limit order or direct market entry upon price re-entering the Bearish Order Block immediately following the sweep of the equal highs.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly above the highest point of the sweep candle (the "sweep high").
- **Invalidation:** A candle close above the sweep high invalidates the short bias ("If a candle closes above the sweep high, the idea has failed").
- **Take Profit Target:** Target the 50% equilibrium level and the Discount zone (down toward the original swing low of the dealing range).

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **What Retail Traders Get Wrong vs. Smart Money Execution:**
  - *Retail Mistake #1 (Chasing):* Buying green candles inside the Premium zone where the risk-to-reward ratio is highly unfavorable.
  - *Retail Mistake #2 (Early Shorting):* Shorting directly at equal highs with tight stops immediately above, turning themselves into the liquidity required for institutional entries.
  - *Smart Money Edge:* Waiting patiently for the equal highs to be swept before entering inside an unmitigated Order Block.
- **Equal Highs are Liquidity, Not Walls:**
  - Double tops / equal highs are liquidity targets designed to be raided. Never consider resistance confirmed until the sweep and rejection occur.
- **Invalidation Clarity:**
  - Wicks above the Order Block are acceptable if they function as sweeps, but a decisive candle close above the sweep high confirms that buyers retain dominance and cancels the setup immediately.
