# Order Block Entry After a Liquidity Sweep: When to Wait vs When to Enter

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT — Liquidity Sweep combined with Change of Character (CHoCH) and Order Block (OB) mitigation entry.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Traders commonly struggle with timing: after seeing a bullish reversal signal, they panic and chase green breakout candles, often buying the temporary top right before a deep pullback. This strategy solves that dilemma by establishing a strict rule of thumb: **Sweep -> CHoCH -> Wait for Order Block Mitigation -> Enter**. It pairs institutional liquidity grabs with structural confirmation to deliver high-probability, favorable risk-reward entries.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Liquidity Sweep (The Trap):**
   - Price is in a downtrend, printing Lower Highs and Lower Lows (LL).
   - Retail stop-loss orders accumulate below the most recent swing low.
   - Smart money pushes price momentarily below this low, triggering sell-stops to accumulate institutional long inventory, and violently snaps back.
2. **Step 2: Change of Character (CHoCH):**
   - Following the sweep, price expands upward with energetic displacement.
   - A candle prints a decisive **body close above the last lower high**, confirming a Change of Character (CHoCH) and signaling an early structural reversal.
3. **The Patience Filter ("Don't Chase It"):**
   - Chasing the green CHoCH breakout candle is a costly trap; price has already expanded and is heavily primed for a corrective pullback.
4. **Step 3: Identify the Bullish Order Block (OB):**
   - Locate the **last down (bearish) candle** formed right at the sweep base immediately before the impulsive displacement rally that broke structure.
   - Highlight the body and wicks of this candle as the institutional Order Block zone.
5. **Step 4: Retracement & Entry:**
   - Wait for price to pull back and mitigate the Order Block.
   - Enter long when price enters the OB zone.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish reversal out of a swept discount extreme.
- **Entry Trigger:** Limit order or market tap upon price pulling back into the Bullish Order Block zone.
- **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed securely just below the low of the Order Block (or below the structural liquidity sweep low).
- **Trade Invalidation / Edge Cases:**
  - **"No Return = No Trade":** If price rallies directly toward targets without pulling back into the Order Block, **do not chase**. Let the trade go.
  - **"Idea Cancelled" Invalidation:** A full candle body close **below the entire Order Block** completely invalidates the setup, indicating seller dominance.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - Target the buy-side liquidity resting above the old swing high (external range liquidity).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **Breakout Chasing Trap:** Retail traders see the CHoCH expansion candle and FOMO buy at the apex of the leg. Smart money waits for the market to retrace to where institutional orders were initially accumulated.
- **Anatomy of the Order Block:** The valid order block is specifically the *last down candle before the displacement*. If that candle did not create displacement or break structure, it is not an institutional order block.
- **Discipline of Invalidation:** If price slices completely through the order block and closes below it, cut the trade or cancel limit orders; never hold hoping for a double bottom.
