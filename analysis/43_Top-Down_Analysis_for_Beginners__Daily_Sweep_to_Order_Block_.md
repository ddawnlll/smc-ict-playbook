# Top-Down Analysis for Beginners: Daily Sweep to Order Block Entry

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Institutional Multi-Timeframe Alignment (Top-Down Analysis), Previous Day Liquidity Sweeps (PDL/PDH), and Lower-Timeframe Order Block Refinement.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Trading a single timeframe creates high vulnerability to market noise, false breakouts, and counter-trend traps.
  - Institutional trading algorithms orchestrate order flow across a synchronized 3-tier timeframe hierarchy:
    1. **Daily (1D - Macro Narrative & Liquidity):** Dictates institutional bias via sweeps of key session/daily liquidity pools (Previous Day High / Low).
    2. **4-Hour (4H - Intermediate Structure & Defense):** Confirms range behavior and structural absorption/rejection around the macro liquidity level.
    3. **15-Minute (15M - Execution & Refinement):** Pinpoints the precise Order Block and entry trigger with minimal risk exposure.
  - The strategy captures high-probability, asymmetric reversal expansions (+3R or greater) by synchronizing a Daily liquidity sweep with an M15 Order Block mitigation.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

### Step 1: Daily Chart (1D) — The Macro Liquidity Sweep
1. Mark the Previous Day High (PDH) and Previous Day Low (PDL) on the daily chart.
2. Resting liquidity (stop losses and breakout orders) clusters heavily just outside these daily extremes.
3. Observe price dipping below yesterday's low (PDL), triggering sell stops, and immediately reclaiming back inside the prior day's range (a **Daily Liquidity Sweep**).
4. **Bias Formulation:** The purge of sell-side liquidity signals that institutional sellers have exhausted liquidity and smart money is accumulating. Shift directional bias to **Longs**.

### Step 2: 4-Hour Chart (4H) — Structural Absorption
1. Transition to the 4-Hour timeframe to evaluate price action at the swept level.
2. Locate the established 4H consolidation range around the sweep low.
3. Confirm that price poked out beneath the range low and rapidly closed back inside with a clear rejection wick, demonstrating active institutional buyer defense of the zone.

### Step 3: 15-Minute Chart (15M) — Structural Displacement & Order Block
1. Zoom into the 15M execution timeframe.
2. Await an aggressive upward push displaying institutional displacement.
3. Identify the **Bullish Order Block (OB)**: defined as the last bearish (down) candle immediately preceding the explosive upward impulse.
4. Mark the Order Block zone across the body/range of this down candle.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation/reversal after a confirmed Daily sell-side liquidity sweep and 4H range defense.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Set a limit order or enter on the market as price pulls back to retest the upper boundary or equilibrium (50%) of the 15M Bullish Order Block.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly below the low of the 15M Order Block (and the underlying sweep wick).
- **Invalidation:** A 15M candle body closing below the low of the Order Block invalidates the trade immediately.
- **Take Profit Targets:**
  - **TP1 (Local Liquidity):** The nearest 15-minute swing high (ideal for de-risking and taking partial profits).
  - **TP2 (Macro Liquidity / Trend Target):** The major 4-Hour range high (delivering a minimum +3R return relative to initial risk).

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "Single Setup Alone is Not a Signal" Rule:** An isolated 15M order block is fragile if traded without higher-timeframe alignment. The high win-rate edge emerges *only* when the 15M block originates directly from a Daily liquidity sweep.
- **Wick Reclaim vs. Daily Breakdown:** If the Daily candle body closes strongly below yesterday's low rather than sweeping and reclaiming inside, the bias remains bearish. Do NOT anticipate a sweep until the reclaim is confirmed.
- **Displacement Requirement:** On the 15M timeframe, the impulse leaving the order block must be sharp and authoritative; slow, overlapping candles indicate lack of institutional backing.
- **Asymmetric Risk Management:** Risking 1R to achieve a conservative 15M target (TP1) and a primary 4H swing target (TP2 at 3R) provides robust positive expectancy even during moderate win-rate regimes.
