## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Pure Price Action Candlestick Anatomy for Trend Exhaustion (Body Size, Wick Rejection, and Inability to Close Higher).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Most retail traders rely on lagging oscillators (RSI, Stochastics, MACD) to spot overbought conditions. 
  - Institutional price reading extracts exhaustion clues directly from candlestick geometry without any indicators.
  - Three visual anomalies signal that buyers are losing conviction:
    1. **Body Size Contraction:** Momentum velocity decays.
    2. **Upper Wick Lengthening:** Overhead supply absorbs upward drives.
    3. **Failure to Close Above Prior Highs:** Inability to achieve new auction highs.
  - Crucially: **"Tired does not mean down."** Exhaustion is merely a warning condition; trade execution requires an explicit structural break (CHoCH / MSS).

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

1. **Clue 1: Decreasing Candle Body Size (Momentum Decay):**
   - During healthy expansion, bullish candles exhibit large, dominant real bodies.
   - As price nears an overhead supply or resistance level, consecutive bullish bodies progressively shrink. This visual compression demonstrates that buyers are committing less capital per unit of time.
2. **Clue 2: Elongating Upper Wicks (Supply Influx):**
   - Upper wicks represent intra-period price rejection.
   - When successive candles print increasingly prominent upper wicks, it indicates that limit sellers are active above and market buying is being absorbed before the candle closes.
3. **Clue 3: Failure to Close Above the Prior Candle's High (Stall):**
   - In a trending regime, each candle typically closes above the preceding candle's high.
   - When a candle fails to close above the high of the candle before it, upward price discovery has officially stalled.
4. **Step 4: The Resistance Confluence Check:**
   - Observe when all 3 clues align concurrently at a significant structural resistance zone. 
   - Note: This is an alert / pre-condition, **not** a sell signal.
5. **Step 5: The Execution Trigger (Structural Shift):**
   - Await the signal: Price must break down and achieve a full candle body close below the **last higher low** of the prevailing micro-trend.
   - This marks a definitive Change of Character (CHoCH).

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Market Bias & Regime:** Bearish reversal following an exhausted uptrend into resistance.
- **Entry Trigger:** 
  - Enter short on the close of the candle that breaks and closes below the last higher low (or on a shallow retest of the broken level/micro-FVG).
- **Stop Loss Placement:** 
  - Placed strictly above the highest swing high formed during the exhaustion cluster.
- **Invalidation Condition (Cancellation Rule):** 
  - If at any point price prints a candle close **above the highest high**, buyers have regained control, the exhaustion warning is cancelled, and short ideas are aborted.
- **Profit Target (Take Profit):** 
  - Target key liquidity resting beneath the base of the rally (previous swing lows / equal lows), yielding an asymmetric 1:2 to 1:3 R:R.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **The "Tired $\neq$ Short" Trap:**
  - The fatal retail mistake is opening short positions simply because candles look small or have wicks. Markets can remain "tired" and drift upwards or consolidate sideways for extended periods. Never sell until the last higher low is broken with a decisive candle close.
- **The Triad Rule (Body, Wick, Close):**
  - High probability occurs only when all three clues are present simultaneously:
    1. Body getting smaller.
    2. Upper wick getting longer.
    3. Close failing to breach prior high.
- **Strict Invalidation:**
  - Institutional setups maintain rigid boundaries. A single candle close above the highest high invalidates the entire exhaustion hypothesis immediately.
