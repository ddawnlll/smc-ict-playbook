# Price Is Back in the FVG: Now What?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Fair Value Gap (FVG) Retest Dynamics & Candle Close Filtration (ICT Imbalance Theory & Consequent Encroachment).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Fair Value Gaps (3-candle price imbalances) are widely used as institutional entry zones. However, when price returns to an FVG, traders frequently panic when price cuts deeply into the zone or struggle to determine whether the imbalance will provide support or collapse entirely.
  - The critical error is focusing on the depth of price penetration rather than how the retest candle closes.
  - **"Do not ask if price came back. Ask how it closed."** Wicks measure price discovery and liquidity collection, while candle bodies reflect institutional consensus and order flow defense.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Identify the 3-Candle Imbalance (Step 1: The Gap):**
   - Identify an impulsive displacement candle (Candle 2) that creates an open void between the high of Candle 1 and the low of Candle 3.
   - Outline the zone as a bullish Fair Value Gap (FVG) and mark its exact 50% midpoint (**Consequent Encroachment / CE**).
2. **Observe the Return into the Imbalance (Step 2: The Return):**
   - Price initiates a corrective retracement back into the gap.
   - Retracements manifest in three standard depths:
     - *Top-Edge Touch:* Shallow retest showing extreme trend velocity.
     - *50% Level (CE):* Standard institutional equilibrium test.
     - *Full Gap Fill:* Deep retest touching the lower boundary of the void.
3. **Analyze the Candle Close Behavior (Step 3: The Close / The Trap):**
   - *The Retail Trap:* Many traders assume that if price dips deep into the lower half of the gap, the setup has failed.
   - *The Institutional Rule:* Depth does not constitute the signal. Inspect the closing price:
     - If a candle's wick plunges through the 50% level or deeper, but the candle **closes with its real body back above the 50% level**, the gap has officially **held**.
     - This price action represents aggressive absorption: smart money absorbed retail market sell orders at a discount and drove price higher before the candle close.
4. **Confirm Buy-Side Expansion:**
   - The subsequent candle confirms institutional sponsorship by pushing strongly upward and away from the gap, validating that buyers have assumed control.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish trend continuation following institutional displacement.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - **Confirmed Entry (Recommended):** Enter long on the close of the retest candle once it confirms rejection by closing its body above the 50% Consequent Encroachment (or on the open of the following bullish candle).
  - **Zone Limit Entry:** Limit buy order at the 50% CE level, requiring strict body-close monitoring.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed safely below the bottom boundary of the FVG (the high of Candle 1) or below the wick low of the retest candle.
- **Invalidation:**
  - A decisive candle body close **below the entire FVG** immediately invalidates the trade setup. The imbalance has failed and will likely invert into resistance.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The swing high formed prior to the retracement into the FVG (internal liquidity).
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major external buy-side liquidity (BSL), equal highs, or higher-timeframe resistance targets.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **"Wicks Lie, Bodies Tell the Truth":**
  - Institutional algorithms often pierce deep into an FVG with long wicks to trigger aggressive retail stop orders and fill institutional limit orders. As long as the real candle body respects the 50% midpoint, the structural bias remains intact.
- **The Invalidation Delineation:**
  - A wick passing through the gap is acceptable; a candle *closing* below the bottom boundary is lethal. Once a candle body closes outside the gap, the premise is broken—exit immediately.
- **Consequent Encroachment (50% Midpoint) Rule:**
  - The 50% line of an FVG serves as the true institutional battleground. Respect of the 50% level on a closing basis is the single highest-probability clue that an imbalance will sustain continuation.
