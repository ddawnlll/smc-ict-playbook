# Order Block Sell Setup Explained: Break of Structure + Inducement

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT — Bearish Order Block execution fueled by Break of Structure (BOS) and Inducement (IDM) liquidity sweeps.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Many traders attempt to sell every order block they find after a downtrend move, only to get stopped out because they trade before retail liquidity is engineered. This strategy presents a structured four-part institutional framework: **BOS -> Inducement Formation -> Inducement Sweep -> Bearish Order Block Mitigation**. By requiring the market to first hunt early sellers' stop losses (inducement), smart money secures the liquidity necessary to defend the higher-placed order block.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Break of Structure (BOS):**
   - Price establishes a notable swing low.
   - An energetic downward impulse pushes through that level, with a candle printing a definitive **body close below the previous swing low**.
   - This registers an official Break of Structure (BOS), indicating that institutional sellers are actively driving market direction.
2. **Step 2: Identify Inducement (IDM):**
   - As the drop pauses and initiates a minor bounce, identify the **first small swing high** formed during or immediately following the drop.
   - This micro high serves as **Inducement**: early, impatient retail sellers place short trades here with their stop-losses clustered directly above this level.
   - Smart money will typically drive price into this zone to sweep those resting buy-stops.
3. **Step 3: Locate the Premium Order Block (OB):**
   - Located *above* the inducement level sits the unmitigated **Order Block**.
   - Identify the **last green (bullish) candle** before the impulsive displacement drop that created the original BOS.
   - Project this candle's range across the chart as the primary institutional sell zone.
4. **Step 4: Inducement Sweep and OB Entry:**
   - Price rallies upward, sweeps clean through the Inducement level (purging early sellers' stops), and taps into the lower edge / body of the Order Block.
   - Trigger the short entry upon mitigation of the Order Block.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Strongly **Bearish / Short** (trend continuation following structural breakdown).
- **Entry Trigger:** Limit order or market execution at the lower boundary (proximal line) of the Bearish Order Block once Inducement is swept.
- **Stop Loss Placement:** Placed safely above the highest point / upper boundary (distal line) of the Order Block.
- **Trade Invalidation:**
  - If price pushes aggressively through the Order Block and prints a full candle close above the block's high, the idea is immediately **failed/invalidated**.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - Primary target is placed at the **newest swing low** created by the prior impulse (targeting sell-side liquidity resting below the external low).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Inducement Distinction (IDM vs. OB):** The most common retail mistake is shorting at the first pullback high (the inducement). Because institutions need liquidity, that first level is engineered specifically to be broken. Never enter short at the inducement; wait for the inducement to be taken out before selling at the true Order Block above it.
- **BOS Close Confirmation:** The initial break of structure requires a full candle body close below the prior swing low. A wick below is merely a liquidity sweep of the low, which could lead to an upward reversal rather than a trend continuation.
- **Clean Invalidation:** The upper edge of the Order Block is a non-negotiable risk barrier. If price breaches and closes above the block, institutional supply has failed, and holding onto the trade is high risk.
