# 6 Reversal Chart Patterns

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Classical Structural Chart Reversals & Neckline Breakout Confirmation Mechanics.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Classic chart patterns (Double Tops/Bottoms, Triple Tops/Bottoms, Head & Shoulders) map the transition of control between buyers and sellers over multi-swing horizons.
  - Rather than anticipating the reversal early while price is still oscillating between boundaries, institutional traders require structural confirmation at the **neckline**.
  - **The Golden Universal Rule:** Across all six patterns, a simple touch or wick through the neckline is completely invalid; an authentic institutional reversal strictly requires a decisive candle **close** beyond the neckline.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

### Pair 1: Double Top & Double Bottom
1. **Double Top (M-Pattern - Bearish Reversal):**
   - **Leg 1:** Strong bullish rally to Top 1.
   - **Retracement:** Pullback to a swing low, defining the horizontal **neckline**.
   - **Leg 2:** Secondary rally failing at the exact level of Top 1 (Top 2), demonstrating exhaustion of buyer momentum.
   - **Confirmation:** Candle decisively closes *below* the neckline.
2. **Double Bottom (W-Pattern - Bullish Reversal):**
   - **Leg 1:** Bearish drop to Bottom 1.
   - **Retracement:** Counter-rally forming an intermediate peak, setting the **neckline**.
   - **Leg 2:** Second sell-off that halts and holds at the exact level of Bottom 1 (Bottom 2).
   - **Confirmation:** Candle decisively closes *above* the neckline.

### Pair 2: Triple Top & Triple Bottom
1. **Triple Top (Bearish Reversal):**
   - Price tests a key resistance ceiling three separate times (Top 1, Top 2, Top 3).
   - Each successive push fails to break out, signaling persistent absorption by heavy sell limit orders.
   - **Confirmation:** A candle body close *below* the horizontal swing lows (neckline).
2. **Triple Bottom (Bullish Reversal):**
   - Price tests a key support floor three distinct times (Bottom 1, Bottom 2, Bottom 3).
   - Selling pressure weakens progressively upon each test.
   - **Confirmation:** A candle body close *above* the horizontal peak level (neckline).

### Pair 3: Head & Shoulders & Inverse Head & Shoulders
1. **Head & Shoulders (Bearish Reversal):**
   - **Left Shoulder:** Price rallies to a swing high and pulls back.
   - **Head:** Stronger rally reaching a new higher high, followed by a deeper retracement.
   - **Right Shoulder:** A weaker rally that fails to make a higher high, creating a lower high.
   - **Neckline Construction:** Trendline or horizontal level connecting both intermediate swing lows (dips).
   - **Confirmation:** Candle body closes decisively *below* the neckline.
2. **Inverse Head & Shoulders (Bullish Reversal):**
   - **Left Shoulder:** Sell-off to an initial swing low, followed by a bounce.
   - **Head:** Deeper sell-off creating the lowest low of the structure, followed by a bounce.
   - **Right Shoulder:** A shallow sell-off that forms a higher low, proving sellers lack the power to print new lows.
   - **Neckline Construction:** Boundary connecting the two intervening corrective peaks.
   - **Confirmation:** Candle body closes decisively *above* the neckline.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Macro trend reversal upon verified structural transition.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - *Breakout Entry:* Immediately on the close of the candle breaking beyond the neckline.
  - *Retest Entry (Institutional):* Waiting for price to pull back and retest the broken neckline (turning former support into resistance, or former resistance into support).
- **Stop Loss:**
  - *Aggressive:* Placed beyond the right shoulder (for H&S) or the midpoint of the retest candle.
  - *Conservative:* Placed beyond Top 2 / Bottom 2 or beyond the Head extreme.
- **Invalidation:** A candle body closing back inside the pattern range past the neckline immediately invalidates the breakout (identifying a false breakout / trap).
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target:** Classical measured move target (vertical distance from the pattern peak/trough to the neckline, projected from the breakout point).
  - **Secondary Target:** Key opposing structural swing levels or liquidity pools.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "Wick Touch" Trap:**
  - Retail traders routinely jump into positions the moment price pierces or touches the neckline. Institutions exploit this by wicking through the neckline to trigger retail breakout market orders, then snapping back into the range. **A close beyond the neckline is non-negotiable.**
- **SMC Liquidity Perspective on Equal Highs/Lows:**
  - To classical chartists, Double/Triple Tops represent resistance. To SMC traders, these equal highs are prime **buy-side liquidity pools**. High-probability entries occur either when price sweeps above the double tops first before breaking down, or when a confirmed neckline body close confirms that smart money has established their position.
- **Symmetry & Volume Clues:**
  - In Head & Shoulders, the right shoulder should ideally print with lower volume than the left shoulder and head, confirming waning participant enthusiasm before the neckline break.
