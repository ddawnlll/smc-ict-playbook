# How to Trade Liquidity: Sweep, Fair Value Gap & Entry (Beginner)

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT — Multi-timeframe Liquidity Sweep combined with Fair Value Gap (FVG) displacement and candlestick confirmation.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Beginners frequently learn various textbook liquidity patterns (equal highs, internal range liquidity, swing lows) but get repeatedly stopped out trying to trade every minor wick on a single timeframe. This strategy presents a systematic, multi-timeframe framework connecting Higher-Timeframe (4H) liquidity pools with Lower-Timeframe (15M) imbalance mitigation and bullish confirmation.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Identify 4H Higher-Timeframe Liquidity:**
   - On the 4-Hour chart, identify a prominent structural **Swing Low** (or Equal Lows).
   - Recognize that institutional stop orders (sell-stops / liquidity) cluster directly beneath this level.
   - Wait patiently for price to drive down and sweep below the 4H level.
2. **Step 2: Drop to 15M and Confirm the Liquidity Sweep:**
   - Switch to the 15-Minute chart.
   - Price must puncture beneath the 4H swing low, purge the resting liquidity, and immediately reject back upward, closing with a candle body back *above* the swept level.
3. **Step 3: Locate the 15M Fair Value Gap (FVG):**
   - Following the sweep, identify the displacement impulse leg.
   - Verify a clean 3-candle imbalance (FVG) where open space exists between the high of Candle 1 and the low of Candle 3.
4. **Step 4: Await Retracement Tap:**
   - Do not chase the displacement.
   - Wait for price to pull back and tap directly into the 15M Fair Value Gap zone.
5. **Step 5: Candlestick Confirmation Entry:**
   - Inside or upon tapping the FVG, wait for price to print a **Bullish Engulfing Candle** confirming that institutional buyers are defending the imbalance.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish reversal at major HTF discount / key liquidity pools.
- **Entry Trigger:** Confirmed close of the **Bullish Engulfing Candle** immediately following the tap of the 15M Fair Value Gap.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly below the Fair Value Gap zone (or conservatively below the absolute low of the 15M liquidity sweep).
- **Invalidation:** A candle body closing below the Fair Value Gap invalidates the trade setup ("Close below = idea off").
- **Take Profit Target:** The next major opposing liquidity pool (e.g., 4H Swing High, Equal Highs, or buy-side liquidity).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Single-Timeframe Liquidity Trap:** Retail traders mark internal liquidity on noisy 1-minute or 5-minute charts and get chopped to pieces. High-probability sweeps MUST be anchored to major Higher Timeframe levels (4H swing points).
- **The Retracement Discipline:** Beginners often panic-buy the green displacement candle immediately after the sweep. Professional execution requires waiting for the market to retrace into the Fair Value Gap.
- **Engulfing Confirmation Filter:** Rather than placing a blind limit order inside the FVG (which risks getting run over if momentum continues downward), wait for the bullish engulfing candle to verify buyer presence.
