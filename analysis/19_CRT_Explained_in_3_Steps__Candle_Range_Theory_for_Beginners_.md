## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Candle Range Theory (CRT) & Institutional Range Liquidity Cycling.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Standard technical analysis evaluates support/resistance across multi-candle horizontal swing structures. 
  - **Candle Range Theory (CRT)** treats a single higher-timeframe candle (e.g., 1-Hour / 1H) as an autonomous auction range containing its own internal liquidity pools.
  - Retail stop losses predictably accumulate immediately above the high (**CRT High**) and beneath the low (**CRT Low**) of that reference candle.
  - Smart money systematically engineers an intra-period manipulation (a liquidity sweep) below one boundary to fuel aggressive expansion across the internal range toward the opposite extreme.

```
       1H CANDLE (THE RANGE)                 5M EXECUTION FLOW (THE SWEEP & REVERSAL)
       
       ----------------- CRT High  ==========================================> FULL TARGET (CRT High)
       |               |                                                     /
       |               |                                      [50% Midline] / (TP1 First Stop)
       |               |                                                   /
       |               |                                                  /
       ----------------- CRT Low   --------------------------------------/---- CRT Low
                                          \                          / [Closes Back Inside]
                                           \________[Sweep]_________/ (5M Liquidity Purge)
                                                    ===> LONG ENTRY
```

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

1. **Step 1: Define the Reference Range (HTF Anchor):**
   - Select a closed higher-timeframe candle (e.g., previous 1-Hour candle).
   - Mark the absolute wick high as **CRT High** (Buy-Side Liquidity).
   - Mark the absolute wick low as **CRT Low** (Sell-Side Liquidity).
2. **Step 2: Monitor Lower Timeframe Execution (The Sweep):**
   - Switch to the lower timeframe (e.g., 5-Minute / 5M chart) during the progression of the subsequent hour.
   - Observe price dipping below the **CRT Low**, triggering retail sell stops and breakout shorts.
   - Look for the selling pressure to immediately dissipate once liquidity is absorbed.
   - Confirm that price snaps back upward and prints an intra-hour close **back inside the range** (above CRT Low). The external penetration is confirmed as a liquidity sweep.
3. **Step 3: Define Opposing Targets:**
   - Once the sweep of one extreme is verified and accepted back into the range, order flow rotates toward opposing resting liquidity.
   - **Target 1 (TP1):** The range midpoint (50% equilibrium level of the 1H candle).
   - **Target 2 (TP2 - Full Target):** The opposite boundary (**CRT High**).

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Market Bias & Regime:** Mean-reversion to opposite boundary expansion following a false breakout / liquidity raid.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Enter long upon a 5M candle closing back above the CRT Low following the sweep (or limit order on a micro-retest of the reclaimed CRT Low).
- **Stop Loss Placement:**
  - Placed strictly below the lowest wick print of the 5M liquidity sweep.
- **Invalidation Condition:**
  - If a 1H or sustained 5M candle body **closes outside the range** (below the CRT Low), the sweep has failed and turned into genuine trend continuation; cancel/exit the long idea immediately.
- **Profit Targets:**
  - **TP1 (Partial):** 50% Equilibrium level (middle of the 1H candle).
  - **TP2 (Full Target):** Opposing CRT High.
- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R):**
  - Typically 1:2.5 to 1:4 R:R due to the tightly contained stop beneath the sweep low relative to the entire 1H candle range.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **Wick Sweep vs. Acceptance:**
  - The critical distinction in CRT is **wick vs. body close**. Price is permitted to wick below CRT Low, but if the candle body accepts and closes below the line, the setup is dead. Institutional control is evidenced by the rapid reclaim back into the body.
- **Two-Timeframe Harmony (1H vs. 5M):**
  - The 1H provides the macro boundaries; the 5M provides surgical entry confirmation. Never try to trade CRT on a single timeframe without monitoring both the boundary container and internal micro-structure.
- **Symmetric Application:**
  - The exact inverse applies for short setups: sweep of CRT High $\rightarrow$ reclaim below CRT High $\rightarrow$ short entry targeting the 50% midpoint and CRT Low.
