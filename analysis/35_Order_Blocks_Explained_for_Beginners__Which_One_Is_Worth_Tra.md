# Order Blocks Explained for Beginners: Which One Is Worth Trading?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & Institutional Order Block Filtration (Comparative Analysis of 4 Order Block Types).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Retail traders commonly label any final bearish candle before an upward rally as an "order block" and attempt to buy every pullback. This naive approach results in high drawdown and frequent stopped-out trades.
  - Institutional market makers only leave defensible order blocks when specific preconditions are satisfied.
  - By comparing four seemingly similar order block setups, this framework establishes a systematic elimination process to identify the single viable candidate: **Liquidity Sweep + Displacement FVG + Fresh (Unmitigated) Status**.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
The strategy evaluates four distinct order block archetypes to isolate the institutional candidate:

1. **Evaluate Block 1 — The "No Sweep" Flaw:**
   - *Anatomy:* Shows a clean last red candle followed by a rally, but the low of the block never dips below the preceding swing low.
   - *Institutional Reality:* Sell-side liquidity (retail stop orders resting beneath the prior low) remains untouched.
   - *Outcome:* When price returns, institutions do not defend the block; instead, price slices through the zone to purge the uncollected liquidity resting below. Traders placing stops beneath Block 1 get liquidated first.
2. **Evaluate Block 2 — The "No FVG / No Urgency" Flaw:**
   - *Anatomy:* Price successfully sweeps the prior low, but the subsequent upward move consists of small, heavily overlapping candles.
   - *Institutional Reality:* There is no institutional displacement or urgency. No Fair Value Gap (FVG) is created, indicating weak, gradual retail or corrective buying rather than aggressive institutional accumulation.
   - *Outcome:* When price revisits the zone, the lack of institutional interest causes the order block to simply give way and collapse.
3. **Evaluate Block 3 — The "Mitigated Trap" (The Biggest Trap):**
   - *Anatomy:* Possesses both a valid liquidity sweep and aggressive displacement leaving an FVG. However, price has already retested/touched the zone once prior to the current approach.
   - *Institutional Reality:* The initial retest already triggered and absorbed the bulk of institutional unfilled limit orders (mitigation).
   - *Outcome:* The second visit encounters depleted order flow. Retail traders expecting a repeated bounce are trapped as price breaks through the weakened level.
4. **Evaluate Block 4 — The Pristine Setup (All 3 Criteria Met):**
   - **Criteria 1 (Liquidity Sweep):** Price purges the prior swing low, extracting sell-side liquidity.
   - **Criteria 2 (Displacement & FVG):** An explosive expansion candle creates an unfilled Fair Value Gap, signaling urgent institutional sponsorship.
   - **Criteria 3 (Fresh / Unmitigated):** Price has not returned to the block since its inception; the institutional order volume remains intact.
   - *Result:* Block 4 is the only setup that passes institutional verification.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation / reversal aligned with the displacement impulse following sell-side liquidity extraction.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Limit order placed at the proximal boundary (open or wick high) of the unmitigated Block 4 order block.
  - Confluent entry can be taken within the overlapping Fair Value Gap.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed strictly below the swept swing low (the low of the order block formation).
- **Invalidation:**
  - A decisive candle close below the order block zone immediately cancels the trade idea. Do not hold through a closed body breach.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The swing high formed at the crest of the displacement impulse (buy-side liquidity).
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major external high-timeframe structural swing highs or opposing unmitigated supply/bearish order blocks.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "Mitigated Zone" Trap:**
  - Retail traders falsely assume that because a level worked once, it will work again. In SMC order flow, each subsequent touch drains resting limit orders, exponentially increasing failure probability.
- **Velocity as Proof of Institutional Presence:**
  - A sweep without velocity (Block 2) is invalid. Institutional accumulation produces immediate, one-sided repricing (displacement candles with FVGs). Slow, overlapping consolidation signals low conviction.
- **The Liquidity Magnet Rule:**
  - Never trust an order block that leaves obvious equal lows or untaken swing lows immediately beneath it (Block 1). Liquidity acts as a magnet; price will inevitably hunt those stops before any sustained reversal can occur.
- **Execution Filter:**
  - Trade *only* Block 4 conditions. If any of the three pillars (Sweep, Displacement FVG, Freshness) is absent, abstain from entering.
