# What is BOS and CHoCH?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Market Structure Analysis — Break of Structure (BOS) vs. Change of Character (CHoCH).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Market structure is the foundation of institutional trend tracking. Traders frequently confuse BOS and CHoCH, but they represent diametrically opposed structural messages.
  - **The Defining Distinction is Direction Relative to Trend:**
    - **BOS (Break of Structure):** A structural break that occurs **with the trend**, confirming momentum continuation and reinforcing the prevailing market bias.
    - **CHoCH (Change of Character):** A structural break that occurs **against the trend**, marking the first time price violates a protective swing point and signaling a potential trend reversal.
  - **The Golden Rule:** Both BOS and CHoCH strictly demand a **candle body close** beyond the structural level; a wick through a level is never a structural break—it is merely a liquidity sweep.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Analyze Market Context in an Uptrend:**
   - Price establishes a swing high, pulls back to form a higher low, and resumes its upward rally.
2. **Identify Bullish BOS (Trend Continuation):**
   - Price rallies through the prior swing high and achieves a **candle body close above that swing high**.
   - This constitutes a **Break of Structure (BOS)**, creating a new Higher High (HH) and confirming bullish continuation.
3. **Recognize Pre-Reversal Exhaustion (Failure Swing):**
   - Price pulls back from the HH and attempts to rally again, but **fails to reach or exceed the previous high**, printing an early Lower High (LH).
4. **Identify Bearish CHoCH (Trend Reversal):**
   - Price rolls over aggressively from the LH and drives downward through the most recent Higher Low.
   - A **candle body closes below the most recent higher low**.
   - Because price broke a swing point **against the trend** for the first time, this is officially a **Change of Character (CHoCH)**, signaling that the uptrend has terminated and a downtrend has begun.
5. **Analyze Downtrend Mechanics (The Inverted Mirror):**
   - **Bearish BOS:** Price in a downtrend rallies into a lower high, then drops to achieve a **candle body close below the previous swing low**, confirming downtrend continuation.
   - **Bullish CHoCH:** Price in a downtrend reverses and achieves a **candle body close above the most recent lower high**, breaking against the downtrend and signaling a bullish trend reversal.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:**
  - **Bullish Regime:** Align with higher timeframe bullish BOS; look for pullbacks to buy.
  - **Bearish Regime:** Align with higher timeframe bearish BOS; look for rallies to sell.
  - **Reversal Regime:** When a validated CHoCH prints, shift bias from trend-following to counter-trend/reversal positioning.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - **Following a BOS:** Do not chase the breakout candle. Wait for a corrective retracement back into the discount zone (for longs) or premium zone (for shorts) tapping an unmitigated Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Order Block (OB).
  - **Following a CHoCH:** Enter on the retest of the origin supply/demand zone that produced the impulse candle body close across the broken swing point.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - **Pro-Trend Trade (Post-BOS):** Placed protected beneath the structural swing low (in an uptrend) or above the structural swing high (in a downtrend) that generated the BOS.
  - **Reversal Trade (Post-CHoCH):** Placed strictly beyond the extreme swing pivot (the sweep high before a bearish CHoCH or the sweep low before a bullish CHoCH).
- **Invalidation:**
  - For a BOS continuation: If price retraces and prints a candle body close beyond the swing origin, the leg is invalidated.
  - For a CHoCH: If price fails to respect the retest and immediately re-breaks back above/below the reversal origin, the CHoCH was a false shift and the setup is canceled.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Post-BOS Target:** The next major swing high/low extension target or external liquidity pool.
  - **Post-CHoCH Target:** Opposing structural swing lows/highs created during the prior exhausted trend leg.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "Wick vs. Body" Trap (The #1 Mistake):**
  - "Both need a candle close, never just a wick." Retail traders mistakenly mark a BOS or CHoCH when a wick pierces a swing high or low. 
  - Institutional reality: A wick that breaches a level and closes back inside is a **liquidity sweep / stop hunt**, not structural progression. Treating a wick sweep as a BOS often traps retail breakout traders into entering right before a violent reversal.
- **The Failure Swing Clue:**
  - A high-probability CHoCH is frequently preceded by a failure swing (inability to print a new HH in an uptrend, or new LL in a downtrend). When price forms a Lower High in an uptrend, the subsequent break of the Higher Low carries exponentially higher reversal probability.
- **Internal vs. External Structure:**
  - Ensure the swing high/low being broken is a true structural pivot rather than minor minor sub-candle noise inside an ongoing range.
