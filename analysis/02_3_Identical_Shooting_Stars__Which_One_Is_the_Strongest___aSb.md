# 3 Identical Shooting Stars: Which One Is the Strongest?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Multi-Timeframe Candlestick Deconstruction, Internal Liquidity Sweeps, and Micro Change of Character (CHoCH / Break of Structure).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - On a high timeframe (HTF, such as the 1-Hour / H1 chart), a Shooting Star candle is conventionally interpreted as a single-candle bearish reversal signal characterized by a long upper wick and a small body near the lows.
  - When comparing three visually identical H1 shooting stars (identical open, high, low, and close), their probability of success varies dramatically based on the internal order flow revealed on the lower timeframe (15-Minute / M15).
  - A valid institutional reversal requires two non-negotiable structural events: **Liquidity Purge (Sweep)** to engineer counter-party liquidity, followed by **Structural Reversal (CHoCH/BOS)** to confirm order flow shift.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Identify H1 Shooting Star at Resistance:**
   - Locate an H1 shooting star forming after an upward trend or approaching a key resistance level.
2. **Deconstruct H1 into M15 Sub-Candles:**
   - Split the 1-hour session into its four 15-minute component candles to analyze internal mechanics.
3. **Classify the Three Internal Sub-Structures:**
   - **Scenario A ("Empty Inside"):** Price rises slowly and drifts down slowly. There is no sweep of prior highs and no break of recent lows ("No Sweep, No Break"). It looks textbook from the outside, but contains zero institutional footprint.
   - **Scenario C ("No Structure"):** Price rapidly pumps and drops within the first 15 minutes, but spends the remaining 45 minutes consolidating sideways. Sellers fade quickly, and no new lower-timeframe market structure is established.
   - **Scenario B ("CHoCH Inside - The Strongest"):**
     - Price drives upward and decisively **sweeps an Equal High (EQH) / Swing High**, hunting buy-side liquidity and triggering early sellers' stop losses.
     - Immediately following the liquidity injection, a large red displacement candle drops aggressively, violently breaking through the last M15 higher low.
     - This creates a verified **Change of Character (CHoCH)** inside the shooting star's session.
4. **Confirm the Dual Criteria:**
   - Checkbox 1: **Liquidity Sweep** confirmed (wick extension above clean highs).
   - Checkbox 2: **Break of Structure (CHoCH)** confirmed (candle close below the prior demand low).

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bearish reversal following liquidity exploitation at premium levels.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - **Aggressive Entry:** Market execution immediately upon the M15 candle closing below the last higher low (confirming the internal CHoCH).
  - **Conservative Entry:** Limit short order placed at the mitigation of the unmitigated M15 supply block or Fair Value Gap left by the drop after the sweep.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed strictly above the absolute high of the shooting star (the peak of the liquidity sweep).
- **Invalidation:**
  - A sustained candle close above the high of the shooting star invalidates the setup immediately ("Story B is cancelled, no matter how good it looks").
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The key swing low that initiated the run up into the swept equal high.
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major HTF sell-side liquidity pools (untested lows, trendline liquidity, or major demand zones).

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Retail Trap (Trading Candle Shapes in Isolation):**
  - Retail traders enter short on any upper wick rejection candle, often getting run over because the wick did not engineer liquidity or break internal structure.
- **Why Scenario B is Superior:**
  - Institutional orders require massive counter-party volume to fill large short positions. Sweeping equal highs lures breakout buyers and forces short liquidations (providing massive buy orders to sell into).
  - The subsequent break of the last higher low proves that buyers have no absorption power and sellers now command the auction.
- **Filtering Checklist:**
  - Did the wick purge significant liquidity (e.g., equal highs, session highs)?
  - Did the sell-off cleanly break structural support (CHoCH) on M15?
  - If neither occurred (Scenario A) or momentum dissipated into sideways noise (Scenario C), pass on the trade.
