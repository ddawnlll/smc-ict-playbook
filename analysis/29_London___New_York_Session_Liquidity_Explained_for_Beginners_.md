# London & New York Session Liquidity Explained for Beginners

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT Session Liquidity Mechanics — Exploiting New York session sweeps of London session liquidity pools (Judas Swing / Session Reversal).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Every major trading session leaves behind distinct highs and lows that function as stop-loss accumulation pools:
  - **Asia Session:** Typically consolidates into a tight, defined range.
  - **London Session:** Breaks the Asian range and establishes high-volume session extremes: the **London High** and **London Low**.
  - **New York Session:** Commonly engineers an opening sweep of London's high or low to purge liquidity, trap breakout retail traders, and initiate a deep directional reversal targeting the opposite boundary of the London range.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Mark the Range (Pre-NY Preparation):**
   - Prior to the New York market open, identify and mark the exact **London High** and **London Low** on the chart.
   - Note the retail stop clusters: buy-stops resting above the London High and sell-stops/protective stops resting below the London Low.
2. **Step 2: A Side is Swept:**
   - As the New York session opens, observe price aggressively pushing through one extreme of the London range (e.g., puncturing above the London High).
   - Breakout buyers enter long, and early short sellers get stopped out.
3. **Step 3: Close Back Inside the Range:**
   - Wait for price action to decisively reject the breakout.
   - A candle must print a **body close back inside** the defined London range boundaries, confirming that the move above/below was a liquidity trap rather than genuine trend expansion.
4. **Step 4: Change of Character (CHoCH):**
   - Drop to the execution timeframe and wait for a structural **Change of Character (CHoCH)** (price breaking the most recent higher low that initiated the sweep).
   - Once CHoCH is confirmed, trigger the trade entry.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Session reversal / range rotation. If London High is swept, directional bias is strictly **Bearish / Short**; if London Low is swept, directional bias is strictly **Bullish / Long**.
- **Entry Trigger:** Enter on the confirmed CHoCH following the candle close back inside the London range.
- **Stop Loss Placement:** 
  - For a short: Placed securely above the extreme peak of the New York sweep candle.
  - For a long: Placed securely below the extreme trough of the New York sweep candle.
- **Invalidation Criterion:**
  - **The "Holds Outside" Rule:** If New York pierces the London boundary, closes *outside* the range, and continues to hold above/below the level (printing consecutive closes outside), the reversal idea is **immediately cancelled**. Do not attempt to fight genuine session trend expansion.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target:** The opposite extreme of the London session (e.g., targeting the London Low when shorting off the London High sweep).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Retail Breakout Trap:** Retail traders see the initial New York surge breaking the London High as a breakout signal and buy into institutional supply. Smart money uses that flood of buy orders to fill large short positions.
- **Wick vs. Close Inside:** A mere wick back inside does not fulfill Step 3; the setup demands a definitive candle close back inside the London range to confirm that buyers lacked the volume to sustain price outside the zone.
- **Rigid Invalidation:** If price breaks out and holds outside, smart money is accumulating for a strong trend day. Forcing a reversal trade when price holds outside is the single most common cause of catastrophic losses in session trading.
