# Equal Highs + FVG: Target First, Entry Second

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** ICT / Smart Money Concepts (SMC) — "Target First, Entry Second" Execution Framework utilizing Buy-Side Liquidity (Equal Highs) as the primary draw on liquidity, paired with a Bullish Fair Value Gap (FVG) discount entry.
- **Market Context & Premise:** Retail traders often search for entries without knowing their destination or target. Smart money execution reverses this order: first establish the liquidity magnet (target where resting stops sit), then locate an institutional discount entry mechanism (FVG) to ride price toward that magnet.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Identify the Target (Equal Highs / Buy-Side Liquidity):**
   * Locate two distinct swing highs that top out at virtually the exact same price level.
   * Recognize the structural reality: Above these equal highs rests an engineered pool of orders consisting of retail stop losses (from traders who shorted the double top) and buy-stop market orders (from breakout traders waiting for a push higher).
   * In Smart Money theory, this resting liquidity pool acts as a magnet drawing price upward.
2. **Step 2: Identify the Drop and the Discount Gap (The FVG):**
   * Price drops far below the equal highs, forming a decisive swing low.
   * A strong, fast bullish green candle pushes aggressively back upward, leaving an unmitigated 3-candle Fair Value Gap (the high of Candle 1 before the displacement and the low of Candle 3 after it do not overlap).
3. **Step 3: Wait for the Retest (The Entry Zone):**
   * Do not chase the aggressive green candle upwards toward the target.
   * Patiently wait for price to retrace and dip back down into the open Fair Value Gap.
4. **Step 4: Risk Calibration & Stop Protection:**
   * Place the stop loss just beneath the recent structural swing low formed before the displacement candle.
   * Keep the profit target locked strictly at the equal highs liquidity pool above.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation / mean reversion toward buy-side liquidity above equal highs.
- **Entry Trigger:** Buy limit or market execution when price dips into the Fair Value Gap zone on the 15M chart.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed just below the structural swing low that preceded the displacement green candle.
- **Invalidation:** A candle body closing below the swing low immediately invalidates the trade idea (signaling failure of the bullish imbalance to hold).
- **Take Profit Target:** Directly at the Equal Highs level (targeting the buy-side liquidity pool), delivering an asymmetric risk-to-reward ratio (typically 2R+).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **"Target First, Entry Second" Axiom:** Never enter an FVG trade without first confirming an active, unmitigated pool of opposing structural liquidity (e.g., equal highs, equal lows, session extremes). Without a clear target, price has no objective draw.
- **Wick vs. Close Invalidation:** Intra-candle wicks may probe below the FVG or near the low, but a decisive candle body close below the swing low is the absolute invalidation criterion.
- **Realistic Edge vs. Guarantee:** As highlighted in the video: "It is one popular approach, not a promise." Strict risk management (fixed 1R risk for 2R+ reward) ensures profitability even when individual setups fail.
