# Buy or Sell? (5 Clues: Bullish Breaker Retest)

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts & Technical Confluence (Bullish Breaker Block + Horizontal Support + RSI Oversold Recovery + CHoCH + Dragonfly Doji Rejection).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  Price establishes a bottom via double rejection, transitions structure upward by violating a prior bearish order block (turning it into a bullish breaker), and subsequently pulls back to retest the breaker. This strategy uses five distinct clues to construct a precise, high-probability long setup.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

1. **Clue 1: Formation of a Bullish Breaker**
   - Identify the last up-close (green) candle prior to an impulsive bearish drop (originally a Bearish Order Block).
   - Price subsequently rallies aggressively and closes above this bearish order block.
   - When a bearish order block fails and price closes above it, it flips into a **Bullish Breaker Block**, which acts as high-probability support upon retest.

2. **Clue 2: Double-Bottom Structural Support**
   - At the absolute base of the move, price tests the same price level twice and produces twin bounces.
   - This verified support floor anchors the structural foundation of the reversal.

3. **Clue 3: RSI Under 30 Oversold Recovery**
   - During the formation of the support base, the RSI dips below 30 into oversold territory, followed by a cross back above 30.
   - *Filter Rule:* Oversold alone is not a buy signal (RSI can stay oversold in strong trends), but crossing back above 30 in confluence with structural support confirms waning selling momentum.

4. **Clue 4: Change of Character (CHoCH)**
   - As price rallies out of the base, it prints a decisive candle close above the most recent lower high.
   - This structural break signals that institutional order flow has flipped from bearish to bullish.

5. **Clue 5: Retest Rejection with a Dragonfly Doji**
   - Price retraces back into the Bullish Breaker zone.
   - At the breaker, price prints a **Dragonfly Doji**—a candle featuring a long lower shadow that dips beneath the zone to sweep local liquidity, with the open, high, and close finishing clustered near the top.
   - This indicates buyers absorbed the selling pressure and defended the breaker level.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation / retest entry following structural trend reversal.
- **Entry Trigger:** Buy stop / market entry positioned just above the high of the Dragonfly Doji once confirmed.
- **Stop Loss:** Positioned just below the low of the Dragonfly Doji wick.
- **Invalidation:** A candle close below the stop loss / below the low of the Doji invalidates the setup immediately.
- **Take Profit Target:** Target the recent swing high (the peak of the initial impulse rally), securing approximately a 1:2.4 Reward-to-Risk ratio (2.4R).

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **What Retail Traders Get Wrong vs. Smart Money Execution:**
  - *Order Block Blindness:* Retail traders often attempt to short the old bearish order block, failing to realize it has been invalidated and converted into a bullish breaker.
  - *Indicator Misuse:* Retail traders buy solely because RSI is below 30. Smart money uses RSI recovery purely as a confluence check alongside structural confirmation (CHoCH).
- **Bullish Breaker Confirmation:**
  - An order block is only transformed into a breaker when price closes *above* the block with candle bodies, not merely wicks.
- **Doji Confirmation Rule:**
  - Wait for the candle following the Dragonfly Doji to close above the Doji's high before considering the entry fully confirmed.
