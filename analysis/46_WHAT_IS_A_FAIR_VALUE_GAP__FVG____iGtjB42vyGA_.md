# WHAT IS A FAIR VALUE GAP (FVG)?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT — Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Single-Sided Price Inefficiency (BISI / SIBI).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - When institutional market participants inject aggressive capital into the market, price surges rapidly in one direction, creating an auction imbalance where only one side (buyers or sellers) is matched.
  - This rapid expansion leaves an open pocket or "gap" between surrounding candle wicks.
  - In algorithmic pricing models, markets exhibit a natural tendency to rebalance these single-sided inefficiencies. Price often rotates back into the FVG to re-deliver fair value (mitigation) before resuming the broader directional trend.
  - The video details the anatomical construction of both Bullish and Bearish FVGs and classifies the three primary retest scenarios that dictate trade outcomes.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Locate the 3-Candle Structural Formation:**
   - Every true Fair Value Gap is defined across exactly three consecutive candles:
     - **Candle 1:** The base or preparatory candle.
     - **Candle 2:** The massive expansion/displacement candle (high momentum, long body).
     - **Candle 3:** The follow-through candle that remains extended away from Candle 1.
2. **Measure the Gap Boundaries (Anatomy):**
   - **Bullish FVG (Buy-Side Imbalance):**
     - Lower boundary: Top of **Candle 1's upper wick** (Candle 1 High).
     - Upper boundary: Bottom of **Candle 3's lower wick** (Candle 3 Low).
     - The void between Candle 1 High and Candle 3 Low is covered solely by Candle 2's body; Candle 1 and Candle 3 wicks do not overlap.
   - **Bearish FVG (Sell-Side Imbalance):**
     - Upper boundary: Bottom of **Candle 1's lower wick** (Candle 1 Low).
     - Lower boundary: Top of **Candle 3's upper wick** (Candle 3 High).
     - The void between Candle 1 Low and Candle 3 High is covered solely by Candle 2's downward body.
3. **Project the Mitigation Zone:**
   - Extend the rectangular imbalance zone horizontally across the chart into future price action.
4. **Evaluate the 3 Retest Scenarios:**
   - **Scenario 1 (Partly Filled):** Price retraces partway into the FVG (often to the ~50% median threshold / Consequent Encroachment) and aggressively reverses away. Signals very strong underlying order flow.
   - **Scenario 2 (Fully Filled):** Price retraces through the entire depth of the FVG, tapping the Candle 1 boundary before bouncing. Rebalances 100% of the inefficiency.
   - **Scenario 3 (Gap Failure / Trend Reversal):** Price slices completely through the FVG without pausing, closing beyond Candle 1. "Not every gap holds"—this confirms the gap has failed and momentum has shifted opposingly.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:**
  - **Bullish Bias:** Long setups when a Bullish FVG forms in an established uptrend or following bullish structural displacement.
  - **Bearish Bias:** Short setups when a Bearish FVG forms in a downtrend or following bearish structural displacement.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - **Aggressive Entry:** Limit order placed at the threshold of the FVG (Candle 3 wick level) or at the 50% midpoint (Consequent Encroachment).
  - **Conservative Entry:** Wait for price to enter the FVG (Partly Filled or Fully Filled) and print a lower timeframe rejection candle or change of character (CHoCH) confirming institutional defense.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - **Bullish Setup:** Placed just below the bottom of Candle 1's wick (or below the swing low origin of Candle 2).
  - **Bearish Setup:** Placed just above the top of Candle 1's wick (or above the swing high origin of Candle 2).
- **Invalidation:**
  - A definitive candle body close piercing beyond the opposing boundary of Candle 1 (Scenario 3) immediately invalidates the setup and prompts an immediate exit.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The external swing liquidity high/low created at the termination of Candle 3.
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major unmitigated swing points, equal highs/lows, or higher timeframe liquidity pools.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Retail FOMO Trap:**
  - Retail traders often enter impulsively at the close of Candle 2 or Candle 3, chasing the momentum breakout at the most expensive possible price (buying at premium / selling at discount). Smart money waits for the retrace into the FVG to enter with minimal drawdown and favorable risk-to-reward.
- **The "Not Every Gap Holds" Trap:**
  - Beginners treat every 3-candle gap as an automatic support or resistance level. An FVG formed against the prevailing higher timeframe trend or without structural displacement is prone to Scenario 3 (slicing right through).
- **Partial vs. Full Fill Nuance:**
  - An FVG does not require a full 100% fill to be considered mitigated. If institutional buyers are aggressive, price will only partially fill the gap before rocketing higher. Monitoring price reaction inside the first half of the gap is critical.
