# Is Your Trendline Really Broken?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Price Action & Trendline Mechanics — The "One Close Rule" and structural retest validation to distinguish false breakouts/sweeps from genuine trend reversals.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Trendlines connect the rising swing lows of an uptrend (requiring at least two anchor points, validated by a third touch). When price pierces below an ascending trendline, retail traders prematurely dump positions or short the market, only to be trapped by a liquidity spike. This strategy provides a disciplined 3-step filtration process centered on candle closes and retest dynamics.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Trendline Construction:**
   - Connect Point 1 and Point 2 (rising swing lows).
   - A third touch confirms trendline significance and institutional recognition.
2. **Step 1: Evaluate the Wick (Filtering Noise):**
   - Price temporarily pokes beneath the trendline.
   - If only the wick penetrates the line while the body remains above, classify it as a liquidity spike/noise. The trendline remains fully intact.
3. **Step 2: The Candle Close (The First Warning):**
   - Wait for the candle to officially close.
   - If the candle body closes back *above* the trendline, the line is actively holding.
   - If the candle body closes *below* the trendline, this registers the **First Structural Warning** of potential trend exhaustion.
4. **Step 3: Analyze the Subsequent Candle (Confirmation vs. False Break):**
   - **Confirmed Breakdown:** Price attempts to pull back into the broken trendline from underneath ("retest"), fails to reclaim it, and prints a bearish rejection candle.
   - **False Breakout (Bear Trap):** Price immediately climbs back and closes above the trendline on the next candle, revealing that the dip was an engineered liquidity run.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Trending market facing potential structural termination or false break continuation.
- **Entry Trigger:** 
  - *Bearish Breakdown Entry:* Wait for Candle 1 to close below the trendline, followed by Candle 2 retesting the underside of the trendline and confirming rejection.
  - *Bullish Continuation (Bear Trap):* If the puncture was purely a wick or the next candle closes back above the trendline, enter long targeting trend continuation.
- **Stop Loss:** 
  - For short breakdown: Placed above the swing high of the retest candle.
  - For long continuation: Placed below the lowest point of the penetrating wick.
- **Invalidation:** For short breakdown trades, an immediate close back above the ascending trendline invalidates the breakdown.
- **Take Profit Target:** 
  - For breakdown: Prior key swing lows and horizontal support levels.
  - For continuation: Prior swing highs and upper channel boundaries.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Core Axiom:** *"A wick is noise. A close is a clue. Wait for the close before you call a trendline broken."*
- **The Premature Short Trap:** Novice traders short in real-time the instant price crosses a trendline during an active candle. Intraday volatility often wicks down to sweep stop orders before closing strongly back inside the trend.
- **The Retest Filter:** A single candle close below the trendline is a warning, not a guaranteed reversal. Genuine trend transitions require follow-through and failure to reclaim on the retest.
