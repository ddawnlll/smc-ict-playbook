# Which Fair Value Gap Is the Strongest? FVG Explained for Beginners

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT — Fair Value Gap Selection Filter, 50% Consequent Encroachment (CE), and Multi-Timeframe Imbalance Confluence.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - During strong trending moves, price frequently prints multiple Fair Value Gaps across a single expansion leg (e.g., Gaps 1, 2, 3, and 4).
  - Retail traders struggle with the "multi-gap paradox," often attempting to buy the first gap they see, resulting in repeated stop-outs as price retraces deeper.
  - To pinpoint the single strongest, highest-probability FVG, traders must apply an objective structural filter based on:
    1. How price closes relative to the gap's **50% halfway mark (Consequent Encroachment)**.
    2. Alignment with **Higher Timeframe (Daily) Fair Value Gaps**.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Identify Multiple Stacked FVGs:**
   - Map all 3-candle Fair Value Gaps formed along an impulsive trending impulse (e.g., Gaps 1, 2, 3, and 4 in an uptrend).
2. **Filter 1 — Closed Through (Eliminate Failed Gaps):**
   - Price retraces into **Gap 1** (shallowest gap) and prints a candle body closing completely below it.
   - Gap 1 has failed; eliminate it from the chart immediately.
3. **Filter 2 — Under 50% Rule (Eliminate Weak Gaps):**
   - Price enters **Gap 2** and closes inside it, but **below its 50% halfway mark**.
   - Standard institutional rule: If candle bodies close in the lower half of an FVG, the gap is structurally weak and lacks institutional buyer defense. Remove Gap 2.
4. **Filter 3 — Holds the 50% Middle Mark (Validate the Strong Gap):**
   - Price descends into **Gap 3**.
   - Price tests the 50% halfway mark; a rejection candle dips lower with its wick, but its **candle body close stays firmly above the 50% mark**.
   - Buyers demonstrate active, aggressive defense of the gap's Consequent Encroachment. Gap 3 is marked as the valid institutional level.
   - *(Note: Gap 4 remains untested below; we cannot judge it and focus on active price defense at Gap 3).*
5. **Filter 4 — Higher Timeframe (Daily) Confluence:**
   - Zoom out to the Daily chart.
   - The entire lower timeframe sequence of multiple small gaps forms **one large Daily Fair Value Gap**.
   - Gap 3's exact price band aligns directly with the **50% halfway mark (Consequent Encroachment) of the Daily Gap**.
   - This multi-timeframe nested confluence provides the maximum possible institutional weight.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish trend continuation in an established markup phase, executing at an optimal discount mitigation level.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Enter long near the **50% halfway mark** of Gap 3 after the rejection candle confirms that the candle body close respects the 50% threshold.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed strictly under the lower boundary of Gap 3 (or just beneath the rejection wick).
- **Invalidation:**
  - A candle body close below the bottom of Gap 3 immediately invalidates the bullish continuation thesis ("idea may be off").
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The major swing high established at the peak of the rally before the retracement started.
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Higher timeframe liquidity pools or Fibonacci extension levels.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Shallow FVG Trap (Gap 1 Trap):**
  - Retail traders are conditioned to fear missing the move and buy Gap 1. In deep retracements, the shallowest gap almost always gets sliced through.
- **The 50% Consequent Encroachment (CE) Rule:**
  - An FVG is divided into two halves: the premium half (upper 50%) and the discount half (lower 50%).
  - In a strong bullish FVG, smart money defends the upper half. If price closes below 50% (Gap 2), institutional bids are absent, making long entries low probability.
  - Wicks can pierce beneath 50%, but **candle body closes must remain above 50%**.
- **Multi-Timeframe Macro Alignment:**
  - Lower timeframe gaps often fail in isolation. When a lower timeframe gap (Gap 3) perfectly intersects the 50% equilibrium of a Daily FVG, it transforms into an institutional fortress with exponential probability of success.
