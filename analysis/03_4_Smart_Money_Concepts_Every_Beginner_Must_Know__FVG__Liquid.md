# 4 Smart Money Concepts Every Beginner Must Know (FVG, Liquidity Sweep, BOS/CHoCH, Order Block)

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Foundational Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT Framework: Fair Value Gap (FVG), Liquidity Sweeps, Market Structure (BOS vs. CHoCH), and Order Blocks (OB).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Markets are not moved randomly by retail traders; they are driven by institutional algorithms delivering price toward resting liquidity pools and rebalancing institutional order imbalances.
  - No single concept works in isolation. High-probability trading requires stacking these 4 core building blocks in sequence: Liquidity Sweep $\rightarrow$ Market Structure Shift (CHoCH/BOS) $\rightarrow$ Imbalance Generation (FVG) $\rightarrow$ Order Block Mitigation.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
The full 4-pillar institutional trade progression unfolds across four distinct phases:

1. **Step 1: Liquidity Sweep (The Fuel Injection)**
   - Identify significant liquidity pools, specifically **Equal Highs (EQH)** or **Equal Lows (EQL)** where retail traders cluster stop-loss orders.
   - Price spikes aggressively beyond the level, converting retail stop-losses into high-volume market orders.
   - A valid sweep is confirmed when price extends a long wick beyond the level but **closes back inside/below** the range. This provides large institutional participants the liquidity required to establish massive counter-positions.
   - *Rule:* Never chase the initial wick spike; wait for structural confirmation.

2. **Step 2: Structural Shift (BOS & CHoCH)**
   - In a trending market (e.g., uptrend characterized by higher highs and higher lows), a candle body close above a previous swing high constitutes a **Break of Structure (BOS)**. *Note:* A wick alone does not count as a valid BOS.
   - Mark the structural low ("Must Hold" level) responsible for that BOS.
   - When price breaks and closes decisively below this swing low, a **Change of Character (CHoCH)** is confirmed, providing an authoritative signal of trend reversal.

3. **Step 3: Imbalance Creation (Fair Value Gap - FVG)**
   - The rapid displacement candle that initiates the structural change leaves behind an imbalance.
   - An FVG is a 3-candle sequence where Candle 1's wick and Candle 3's wick do not overlap, leaving an open price void in Candle 2.
   - This unhedged void acts as a magnet for price to return and rebalance institutional orders.

4. **Step 4: Order Block Identification (The Origin of Displacement)**
   - Locate the **Order Block (OB)**:
     - For a bullish setup: The last down-close (red) candle before the aggressive upward displacement that broke structure (BOS).
     - For a bearish setup: The last up-close (green) candle before the violent downward move.
   - *Identification Filter:* A high-quality order block is larger than the candle immediately preceding it.
   - Mark the entire range from the Order Block candle's high to its low to establish the entry zone.

5. **Step 5: Mitigation & Re-entry**
   - Wait patiently for price to retrace back into the Order Block and FVG confluence zone.
   - Wait for a confirmation reversal candle to print inside the zone prior to executing.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Aligned with the direction of the CHoCH and institutional displacement (Bullish after a sell-side liquidity sweep and bullish BOS/CHoCH; Bearish after a buy-side sweep and bearish CHoCH).
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Price returns into the defined Order Block zone (overlapping with an FVG).
  - Trigger entry when price prints a definitive reversal candlestick inside the zone (or limit order at the OB high for bullish / low for bearish).
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed strictly beyond the extreme outer boundary (high/low) of the Order Block zone.
- **Invalidation:**
  - A definitive candle body close outside the opposite boundary of the Order Block zone completely invalidates the setup.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The opposing swing high/low that formed the recent pullback.
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major opposing liquidity pools (equal highs, untested order blocks, or HTF imbalances).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "Wick vs. Close" Rule:**
  - Sweeps are characterized by wicks protruding through key levels followed by closes back within the range.
  - Conversely, true structural breaks (BOS and CHoCH) require full candle body closes beyond the structural pivot. A wick break without a close is often just another liquidity sweep.
- **Order Block Quality Filters:**
  - An order block is only valid if it was directly responsible for a Break of Structure (BOS) and created an unfilled Fair Value Gap (FVG). Random opposite-colored candles without displacement are not order blocks.
  - Size matters: Ensure the order block candle has substantial body size compared to previous consolidation candles.
- **The Confluence Mandate:**
  - Do not trade an FVG or Order Block blindly on its own. Only execute when all four concepts align: Liquidity Sweep $\rightarrow$ Structural Shift (CHoCH) $\rightarrow$ Displacement with FVG $\rightarrow$ Order Block Retest.
