# Fair Value Gap or Breakaway Gap? Check the Third Candle

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / ICT Imbalance Analysis — Differentiating between a standard Fair Value Gap (FVG) and a Breakaway Gap using 3-candle price action anatomy.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** Traders frequently spot a 3-candle imbalance (a large displacement candle sandwiched between two candles leaving an unfilled space between Candle 1's wick and Candle 3's wick) and assume price must immediately retrace to fill it. This strategy introduces a precise mechanical filter—the close of the third candle—to determine whether the imbalance represents a standard Fair Value Gap (likely to see an immediate mitigation/fill) or a Breakaway Gap (signaling violent institutional follow-through and immediate continuation).

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Initial Baseline (Candle 1):** Price forms the first candle, establishing a reference point (e.g., Candle 1's high in a bullish expansion).
2. **Displacement Expansion (Candle 2):** An energetic, large-bodied middle candle expands aggressively away from the base, creating a price imbalance with minimal two-way trading.
3. **Imbalance Formation:** An open space/gap exists between the extreme of Candle 1 (high) and the opposing extreme of Candle 3 (low).
4. **The Third Candle Evaluation (The Core Filter):**
   - **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** Candle 3 fails to close above Candle 2's high. This demonstrates an initial loss of immediate upside momentum, signaling that price is likely to pull back and rebalance the gap zone.
   - **Breakaway Gap:** Candle 3 closes cleanly *above* Candle 2's high. This confirms strong institutional follow-through and aggressive continuation, showing that buyers are unwilling to wait for a discount.
5. **Post-Gap Price Behavior:**
   - For an FVG: Expect an orderly retracement back into the gap zone between Candle 1's high and Candle 3's low to mitigate the imbalance before resuming trend.
   - For a Breakaway Gap: Price is much more likely to continue rocketing forward without offering an immediate deep retracement; any eventual return to the gap happens only after substantial extension.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Directional alignment with the macro trend and higher timeframe market structure. Imbalances must emerge from key liquidity sweeps or consolidations in the direction of the dominant trend.
- **Entry Trigger:** 
  - *Standard FVG:* Wait for price to retrace into the Fair Value Gap zone (tapping the Consequent Encroachment / 50% equilibrium or the upper boundary of the gap at Candle 3's low).
  - *Breakaway Gap:* Do not stand in front of the market or wait for a deep limit fill. Trade aggressive continuation on lower-timeframe re-accumulations or wait for secondary market structure confirmation rather than expecting the gap to fill immediately.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly below Candle 1's low or beneath the swing low / origin of the displacement candle (Candle 2).
- **Invalidation:** A candle body closing fully below the origin of the gap (below Candle 1's high in a bullish setup) invalidates the bullish order flow and turns the gap into an Inversion FVG.
- **Take Profit Target:** External liquidity pools, equal highs (EQH), opposing key swing highs, or higher timeframe structural resistance.

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **Retail vs. Smart Money Trap:** Retail traders treat all imbalances equally and set passive limit orders inside every single gap. On Breakaway Gaps, price leaves them behind, leading to FOMO entries at the very top of extended moves; alternatively, when a Breakaway Gap finally retraces much later, retail traders try to buy what is actually a structural reversal.
- **The "Third Candle Close" Rule of Thumb:** 
  - Close $\le$ Candle 2 High $\rightarrow$ Standard FVG (Expect pullback to gap).
  - Close $>$ Candle 2 High $\rightarrow$ Breakaway Gap (Expect strong continuation, low probability of immediate fill).
- **Classic Charting vs. ICT Terminology:** In traditional technical analysis, a breakaway gap is a true empty space leaving a chart pattern or range; in modern ICT/candlestick terminology, it is identified within continuous candlestick charts when Candle 3 closes above Candle 2's displacement high.
- **Prerequisite Context:** Neither gap is a standalone buy/sell signal. Trend direction, higher timeframe liquidity targets, and overall market structure dictate validity.
