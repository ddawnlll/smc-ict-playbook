# HOW TO USE FIBONACCI RETRACEMENT?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Classical Technical Analysis / Fibonacci Retracement Mechanics — Measuring pullback depth following an impulsive expansion to identify potential reversal zones.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:** After an energetic price rally or drop, the market pulls back. The Fibonacci Retracement tool measures the percentage of that initial impulsive leg that price retraces before attempting continuation. Rather than treating Fibonacci levels as mystical inflection points, the strategy presents a disciplined 4-step framework to identify the swing, plot the tool correctly, monitor price reaction, and filter out low-probability choppy environments.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Step 1: Find the Move (Impulse Identification):** 
   - Locate a clean, unambiguous trending leg.
   - Mark the origin where the rally started (**Swing Low**) and where the rally topped out (**Swing High**). The distance between these points represents the full impulse move ($100\%$).
2. **Step 2: Draw the Tool (Correct Anchoring):**
   - Select the Fibonacci Retracement tool.
   - For an uptrend: Anchor **Click 1** at the Swing Low and drag **Click 2** to the Swing High.
   - Alignment check: $0.0$ sits at the destination (Swing High), and $1.0$ sits at the origin (Swing Low), measuring how much of the prior climb has been surrendered.
3. **Step 3: Read the Key Levels:**
   - $0.236$ ($23.6\%$ retracement) — Shallow pullback (strong momentum).
   - $0.382$ ($38.2\%$ retracement) — Moderate pullback.
   - $0.500$ ($50.0\%$ retracement) — Equilibrium ("half given back").
   - $0.618$ ($61.8\%$ retracement) — Golden ratio retracement zone.
   - $0.786$ ($78.6\%$ retracement) — Deep discount retracement.
4. **Step 4: Watch Price in the Target Zone (The Fib Zone):**
   - Define the primary target area between $0.382$ and $0.618$ (the Golden "Fib Zone").
   - Observe price deceleration as it approaches $0.618$.
   - Wait for tangible confirmation: look for a shift in candle color/anatomy (e.g., strong green reversal candle forming) indicating buyers are actually stepping into the market.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Clear, trending market regime. The strategy is exclusively applicable when there is an obvious unidirectional impulse leg.
- **Entry Trigger:** Reversal confirmation candle within the $0.382 - 0.618$ Fib Zone (e.g., bullish engulfing or strong green close showing buyers defending the level). Never enter blindly with passive limit orders.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly below the $0.786$ retracement or below the origin Swing Low ($1.0$).
- **Invalidation:** Price slicing directly through the $0.618 / 0.786$ levels without deceleration, or a candle close breaking the Swing Low ($1.0$).
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - Primary Target: Prior Swing High ($0.0$ level).
  - Secondary Target: Fibonacci extension levels ($1.272$ or $1.618$).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The Empirical Reality Check (Statistical Backtesting):** Broad backtests on thousands of stocks demonstrate that pullbacks are statistically no more likely to end at $38.2\%$, $50.0\%$, or $61.8\%$ than any random level. Treat Fib levels as **"areas of interest to watch, not a physical law."**
- **The Choppy Range Trap:** Never apply Fibonacci tools inside sideways consolidation or noisy, overlapping chop. If two traders look at the chart and anchor to different arbitrary wicks, the levels will completely diverge and lose any predictive value.
- **Confirmation vs. Prediction:** Novice retail traders place blind limit orders on the $0.618$ line and get run over when price slices straight through. Professional execution demands waiting for exhaustion and buyer/seller entry confirmation.
