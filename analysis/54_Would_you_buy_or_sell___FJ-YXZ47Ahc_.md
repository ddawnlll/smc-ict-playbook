## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview

A fundamental axiom of institutional trading is:
> **"One clue alone is not a signal. Confluence creates the edge."**

This strategy presents a masterclass in systematic short execution by stacking **5 distinct technical clues** across liquidity, structural levels, momentum oscillators, price action shifts, and candlestick retest confirmation into a single high-probability trade plan.

The setup identifies when a bullish expansion exhausts into major resistance, hunts buy-side liquidity, structurally breaks market character (CHoCH), and offers a low-risk entry on the retest of the broken pivot.

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics (The 5 Clues)

```
                       [ CLUE 1: Buy-Side Sweep ]
                       (Poke above & close below)
                                  /\
                                 /  \  ◄── [ CLUE 2: Key Resistance Level ]
                                /    \
   [ CLUE 3: RSI > 70 ]        /      \
   (Drops back below 70)      /        \
                             /          \
  Last Higher Low ──────────*            \
  (Support Level)            \            ▼
                              \         [ CLUE 4: CHoCH ]
                               \        (Candle body closes below Higher Low)
                                \             │
                                 \            ▼ (Retest Leg)
                                  \       [ CLUE 5: Gravestone Doji ]
                                   \      (Long upper wick rejection at broken level)
                                    \           │
                                     \          ▼ [ENTRY TRIGGER]
```

### The 5 Clues in Sequence:

1. **Clue 1: Buy-Side Liquidity Sweep (BSL)**
   - Price pushes aggressively beyond a previous swing high where retail breakout traders enter long and short-sellers have stop orders.
   - Immediately after triggering buy stops, price rejects and **closes back below the swing high**, proving the breakout failed and was merely a liquidity injection.

2. **Clue 2: Key Overhead Resistance**
   - The swept high aligns with a historical, high-timeframe structural resistance level where sellers previously entered the market.
   - Confluence between structural resistance and the sweep increases the likelihood of institutional distribution.

3. **Clue 3: RSI Momentum Exhaustion (Overbought Hook)**
   - The Relative Strength Index (RSI) surges above 70 into overbought territory.
   - Price then curls over and RSI **drops back below 70**, signaling that upward momentum is fading.
   - *Nuance:* RSI alone cannot be shorted (in strong trends it stays overbought); it serves strictly as secondary momentum confirmation.

4. **Clue 4: Change of Character (CHoCH)**
   - Price breaks aggressively downward and prints a definitive **candle body close below the last significant higher low** of the uptrend.
   - *Strict Rule:* A wick penetration does not qualify; a full body close is mandatory to confirm that sellers have seized structural control.

5. **Clue 5: Retest + Gravestone Doji Rejection**
   - Following the CHoCH, price pulls back upward to retest the broken higher low (now flipped from support to resistance).
   - At the retest level, a **Gravestone Doji** forms:
     - Open and close are situated near the absolute low of the candle.
     - Features a long upper wick showing buyers attempted to push higher but were violently absorbed and repelled by institutional sell orders.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

```
[ Retest High / Doji Wick ] ──► STOP LOSS
[ Broken Higher Low Level ] ──► Gravestone Doji Formed
[ Doji Low Breakout ]       ──► SHORT ENTRY
                                       │
                                       │ (Price runs downward)
                                       ▼
[ Previous Swing Low ]      ──► TAKE PROFIT TARGET (~2.5R)
```

### Execution Parameters:

| Component | Execution Parameter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Order Type** | Sell Stop order placed just below the low of the Gravestone Doji (or market sell on candle close) |
| **Trigger Confirmation** | The Gravestone Doji must close and confirm seller rejection at the retest level |
| **Stop-Loss (SL)** | Placed strictly above the high of the retest / Gravestone Doji upper wick |
| **Take-Profit (TP)** | Target the previous swing low formed by the initial impulse leg |
| **Risk-to-Reward (R:R)**| Standard benchmark: ~1:2.5 Risk-to-Reward ratio |
| **Hard Invalidation** | If price prints a candle close above the stop loss level, the setup has officially failed |

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **The Single-Indicator Trap:**
  - Shorting purely because RSI is above 70 is an amateur mistake; strong trends can remain overbought for extended periods. RSI is only operative when paired with structural shifts.
- **The Wick CHoCH Trap:**
  - Ensure the CHoCH is confirmed by a candle body close. Wicks below the higher low frequently represent internal liquidity sweeps before another upward leg.
- **The Confirmation Requirement for Dojis:**
  - A Gravestone Doji at resistance demonstrates rejection, but entering without seeing price break below its low exposes the trader to continuation risk. Entering on the break of the doji low confirms seller dominance.
- **Support-Turned-Resistance (Polarity Flip):**
  - The highest probability short entries occur when the broken structural level (the previous higher low) acts as a ceiling. If price easily reclaims inside the old range, exit immediately.
