# 5 Checks Before an Order Block Entry

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Smart Money Concepts (SMC) / Institutional Order Block Validation.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Order blocks are widely traded by retail SMC traders, but trading every single order block leads to high failure rates and frequent stop-outs.
  - Institutional order blocks only carry high probability when preceded by liquidity absorption and followed by structural market displacement that creates an imbalance (Fair Value Gap).
  - This strategy outlines a rigorous 5-step checklist required to validate a bullish order block prior to executing a long position, filtering out low-probability retail traps.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

### Step 1: Liquidity Sweep ("No Sweep = No Setup")
- Identify a clear, clean swing low holding sell-side liquidity (retail stop-loss orders).
- Price must aggressively sweep below this swing low to engineer liquidity (triggering retail sell stops and filling institutional buy orders).
- The sweeping candle (or immediate successor) must close back above the swept low level, leaving a clear rejection wick below.
- *Strict Rule:* If there is no liquidity sweep, there is no valid setup.

### Step 2: Change of Character (CHoCH)
- Mark the previous internal swing high that preceded the downward sweep.
- Price must exhibit strong upward displacement and produce a full candle body close above this swing high.
- A wick break is insufficient; an institutional body close confirms an authentic shift in short-term market structure (CHoCH).

### Step 3: Identify the Institutional Order Block
- Locate the origin of the displacement: the last red (bearish) candle before the aggressive upward rally.
- In this high-probability model, this is the exact candle that swept the swing low.
- Mark the entire range of this candle (from high wick to low wick) as the high-probability Bullish Order Block.

### Step 4: Fair Value Gap (FVG) Confirmation & Mitigation Check
- **FVG Requirement:** Inspect the rally following the order block. There must be an open Fair Value Gap between the high of Candle 1 (the Order Block) and the low of Candle 3. This proves aggressive displacement and institutional sponsorship.
- **Mitigation Check:** Verify that no subsequent candle has already retested or touched the Order Block. It must remain completely fresh/unmitigated.
- *Rule:* If price has already touched or tapped the Order Block before your planned entry, the setup is spent and disqualified.

### Step 5: Lower-Timeframe / Confirmation Entry Trigger
- Price retraces back into the unmitigated Order Block / FVG zone.
- Do not enter blindly with a limit order at the zone edge without confirmation.
- Wait for price to dip into the zone, reject the level, and print a clear rejection candle with a long wick dipping into the block and a solid bullish close upward.

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation or reversal following sell-side liquidity purge and structural confirmation (CHoCH).
- **Entry Trigger:** Re-test of the unmitigated Bullish Order Block / FVG confluence, confirmed by a rejection wick and a bullish candle close.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed strictly below the low of the Order Block (the absolute swing low created by the liquidity sweep).
- **Invalidation:** A candle body close below the low of the Order Block immediately invalidates the setup; any premature mitigation prior to the planned retest also cancels the order.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target:** Buy-side liquidity resting above the recent swing high (the high created during the CHoCH displacement move).
  - **Secondary Target:** Higher-timeframe liquidity pools or unmitigated bearish inefficiencies above.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **Retail Misconceptions vs. Institutional Reality:**
  - *Retail Error:* Marking every consecutive red candle in a downtrend as an order block and placing limit orders without checking for liquidity sweeps or displacement.
  - *Smart Money Execution:* Order blocks are only valid when they serve an institutional purpose—specifically, absorbing liquidity (sweep) and aggressively breaking structure (CHoCH + FVG).
- **The "Already Mitigated" Trap:**
  - Even if an order block swept liquidity and caused a CHoCH, if a subsequent wick already tapped the block during the displacement sequence, the institutional pending orders have already been filled. Secondary taps are prone to failure.
- **Candle Anatomy Criteria:**
  - The sweeping candle should demonstrate absorption (wick underneath, close back inside the range).
  - The CHoCH candle must close decisively above the swing high, not merely wick through it.
  - The entry candle must show responsive buying (rejection wick confirming that institutional bids are active at the block).
