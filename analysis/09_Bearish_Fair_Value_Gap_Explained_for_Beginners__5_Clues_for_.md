## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Multi-Factor Confluence Short Execution combining classical chart resistance, momentum divergence, Smart Money Concepts (SMC) market structure shift, Fair Value Gap (imbalance), and a 3-candle reversal pattern.
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - Trading a Fair Value Gap in isolation has low probability because price frequently blows through imbalances if high-timeframe order flow or momentum is opposing.
  - This institutional strategy layers **5 distinct technical clues** that systematically transition an asset from an extended uptrend into an asymmetric 1:3 R:R short trade:
    1. Horizontal Resistance rejection.
    2. Bearish RSI Divergence (exhaustion of buyers).
    3. Bearish Fair Value Gap (displacement and institutional selling).
    4. Change of Character / CHoCH (structural shift below higher low).
    5. Evening Star Candlestick Formation inside the FVG (entry trigger).

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics

1. **Clue 1: Identify Resistance Rejection:**
   - Locate an established horizontal resistance level where price has previously reacted and sellers have repeatedly defended the level.
2. **Clue 2: Confirm Bearish RSI Divergence:**
   - Price attempts another push into/around resistance, carving a **higher high (HH)** on the price chart.
   - The Relative Strength Index (RSI) fails to confirm the price action and registers a **lower high (LH)** below the overbought threshold. This divergence signals that the upward momentum is rapidly evaporating.
3. **Clue 3: Spot Institutional Displacement & Bearish FVG:**
   - Following the divergence, price plummets aggressively via an expansive red candle.
   - A 3-candle sequence leaves a **Bearish Fair Value Gap (FVG)**: the open void between the wick low of Candle 1 and the wick high of Candle 3.
4. **Clue 4: Structural Confirmation via CHoCH:**
   - Within that same impulsive drop, price breaks and decisively closes below the most recent swing low (the last higher low of the preceding uptrend).
   - This validates a formal **Change of Character (CHoCH)**, confirming sellers have seized structural control.
5. **Clue 5: Pullback & Re-entry Confirmation (Evening Star):**
   - Price retraces back upward into the newly formed Bearish FVG zone.
   - Inside the FVG, wait for the formation of an **Evening Star pattern**:
     - *Candle 1:* Strong bullish green candle entering the gap.
     - *Candle 2:* Small-bodied pause/indecision candle at the peak.
     - *Candle 3:* Decisive bearish red candle closing deep into (or below) the midpoint of Candle 1's body.

## 3. Trade Execution Plan

- **Market Bias & Regime:** Bearish reversal following an exhausted uptrend into HTF resistance.
- **Entry Trigger:** 
  - Sell Limit placed within the FVG, or a Market Short order upon the closing confirmation of the 3rd candle of the Evening Star pattern inside the FVG.
- **Stop Loss Placement:** 
  - Placed strictly above the upper boundary of the Fair Value Gap (and above the high of the Evening Star pattern).
- **Invalidation Condition:** 
  - If a candle closes above the stop loss level (the top of the FVG), the short idea is immediately invalidated and closed.
- **Profit Target (Take Profit):** 
  - Target the prior swing low formed at the bottom of the initial CHoCH displacement move.
- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R):** 
  - Minimum 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio (3x the risk distance).

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules

- **Why Most Traders Fail Trading FVGs Alone:**
  - Retail traders mark every FVG on the chart and blindly short them, getting run over when price is merely pulling back in a strong bull market.
  - High-probability FVG trading requires **structural validation (CHoCH)** and **exhaustion confluence (Divergence + Resistance)** before the FVG is trusted.
- **The Role of the Evening Star Trigger:**
  - Do not blindly place a short limit at the front edge of the FVG without watching lower timeframe or candle confirmation. The Evening Star proves that buyers failed to fill the gap and aggressive market sellers stepped back in.
- **Rule of Invalidation:**
  - Never widen the stop loss if price pushes through the upper boundary of the FVG. If the FVG is violated with a candle body close, it becomes an Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG) and price is likely targeting higher liquidity.
