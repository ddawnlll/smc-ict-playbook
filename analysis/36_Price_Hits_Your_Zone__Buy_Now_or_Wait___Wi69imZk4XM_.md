# Price Hits Your Zone: Buy Now or Wait?

## 1. Strategy & Concept Overview
- **Core Concept:** Point of Interest (POI) Confirmation Mechanics vs. Blind Execution (Smart Money Concepts / ICT Lower-Timeframe Entry Protocols).
- **Market Context & High-Level Premise:**
  - When price arrives at a higher-timeframe Point of Interest (POI) or key demand zone, retail traders typically make one of two mistakes: they place a blind limit order at the zone's edge, or they enter on the very first green candle.
  - Both approaches carry high failure rates because neither provides structural proof of buyer presence; strong downward momentum can easily blow through unconfirmed zones.
  - Institutional trading demands **confirmation** on lower timeframes (e.g., 1-minute chart) to prove that smart money is actively absorbing supply and defending the zone.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Setup Mechanics
1. **Identify the Higher-Timeframe POI Zone:**
   - Define a high-probability demand zone, order block, or discount FVG on the higher timeframe.
   - Monitor price as it drops into the zone. Do not enter prematurely.
2. **Reject Unconfirmed Triggers (Options A & B):**
   - *Option A (First Green Candle):* Rejected. A single green candle often represents minor profit-taking or momentary pause before downward continuation.
   - *Option B (Blind Limit Order):* Rejected. Possesses zero proof that buyers will defend the level; risk of severe slippage and immediate drawdown.
3. **Wait for Lower-Timeframe Confirmation (Option C):**
   - Drill down to the lower timeframe (e.g., 1-Minute / M1 chart) and monitor for three specific institutional confirmation models:
     - **Confirmation Model 1 (Sweep + Reclaim):** Price dips below a local low inside the POI, sweeping resting retail sell stops. A subsequent candle aggressively closes back above the breached low, confirming a liquidity purge (stop grab) rather than a breakdown.
     - **Confirmation Model 2 (Change of Character / CHoCH):** Price breaks structural order flow by closing a candle above the most recent lower high on the 1-minute chart. This confirms that buyers are taking over order flow. Entry is executed on the retest of the broken level or newly formed demand.
     - **Confirmation Model 3 (Inverse Fair Value Gap / IFVG):** Price drives through a previously formed bearish Fair Value Gap within the zone and closes above it. This inverts the bearish imbalance into a bullish support shelf (IFVG). Entry is taken on the retest of this inverted gap.
4. **Identify the Premier Confluence ("The Best Case"):**
   - The highest probability setup combines Model 1 and Model 2: an internal **Sweep of a minor low** followed immediately by a decisive **CHoCH** (closing above the lower high).

---

## 3. Trade Execution Plan
- **Bias / Market Regime:** Bullish continuation/reversal from a higher-timeframe POI demand zone.
- **Entry Trigger:**
  - Execute on the retest of the CHoCH structural level or retest of the Inverse Fair Value Gap (IFVG) following a confirmed sweep and reclaim.
- **Stop Loss:**
  - Placed strictly under the lowest point of the **sweep wick** (the extreme manipulation low formed within the POI).
- **Invalidation (The Cancel Rule):**
  - A candle close below the entire higher-timeframe POI zone immediately cancels the trade idea. If price violates the boundary with a body close, the setup is dead—**No Trade**.
- **Take Profit Target:**
  - **Primary Target (TP1):** The initial lower-timeframe supply origin or swing high that initiated the drop into the POI.
  - **Secondary Target (TP2):** Major external buy-side liquidity (BSL) or opposing higher-timeframe imbalance.

---

## 4. Key Nuances, Traps & Rules
- **The "First Green Candle" Trap:**
  - Novice traders mistake the appearance of any green candle for an institutional reversal. Without a structural break (CHoCH) or liquidity sweep, this candle is merely a liquidity hook before price prints a fresh low.
- **The Blind Limit Flaw:**
  - Placing blind limits assumes the zone must hold. Professional execution uses zones merely as alerts: price must show its hand before risk is committed.
- **Why Sweep + CHoCH Is Superior:**
  - The sweep extracts liquidity to fuel the move; the CHoCH proves institutional willingness to pay up above prior selling levels. Combined, they offer tight invalidation and asymmetric risk-to-reward ratios.
- **Strict Invalidation Discipline:**
  - Never widen a stop loss or hope for a reversal if the POI zone closes invalid. Once the lower boundary closes red, the institutional premise is nullified.
